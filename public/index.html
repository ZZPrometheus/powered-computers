What we do

Four jobs, done at the bench. Bring in a machine that will not start, runs slowly, or needs more than it has.

  1. SVC-A

    Computer repair

    Desktops that will not boot, crash, freeze, or run hot.

    • Hardware fault diagnosis
    • Windows errors and failed updates
    • Virus and malware removal
    • Data recovery and backup
  2. SVC-B

    Laptop repair

    Cracked screens, dead batteries, and laptops that stopped charging.

    • Screen and keyboard replacement
    • Battery and charging port repair
    • Overheating, fan and thermal paste service
    • Liquid damage assessment
  3. SVC-C

    Upgrades

    Get more out of the machine you already own.

    • SSD swap and fresh operating system install
    • Memory upgrades
    • Graphics card and power supply upgrades
    • Compatibility check before you buy parts
  4. SVC-D

    Custom workstation building

    A machine specified for your work, assembled and tested here.

    • Video editing, 3D, CAD and design
    • Gaming and streaming rigs
    • Office and home business desktops
    • Parts chosen to your budget, with room to upgrade later
